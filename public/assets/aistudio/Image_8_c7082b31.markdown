## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial aesthetic at the intersection of Scandinavian minimalism, Japanese wabi-sabi precision, and contemporary Indonesian tropical architecture. Built for high-end specialty roasters, cultural spaces, and curated lifestyle storefronts, it cultivates a slow-living, tactile atmosphere.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Editorial Quietude:** Layouts draw from publication designâ€”notably *Kinfolk* and *Cereal*â€”emphasizing deliberate whitespace, expansive leading, and quiet structural asymmetry.
- **Architectural Rhythm:** Surfaces replicate natural materials like poured microcement, raw linen, scorched hinoki wood, and matte ceramics.
- **Understated Restraint:** Interactions reject bouncy or flashy transitions. Movement is linear, deliberate, and softly faded, mirroring the quiet ritual of pour-over brewing.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in unbleached paper fibers, rich roasted coffee oils, dry sand, and botanical accents. 

### Palette Application
- **Canvas Base (`#F4EFE7`):** The primary background tone. Replaces stark digital whites with an unbleached parchment texture that softens optical glare.
- **Primary / Mocha Brown (`#8B6848`):** Applied to active navigation states, primary buttons, subheadings, and secondary interaction highlights.
- **Secondary / Deep Olive (`#333D31`):** A grounding architectural green used for select micro-accents, editorial badges, origin callouts, and dark-mode alternative cards.
- **Tertiary / Sand Muted Gold (`#C8A783`):** Reserved for subtle badges, secondary borders, active toggle indicators, and premium metadata marks.
- **Neutral Dark / Dark Espresso (`#211B17`):** The foundation for primary body copy, bold titles, and deep footer panels.
- **Structural Lines (`#E5DDD3`):** High-delicacy border tone used to draw razor-thin dividers without fracturing white space.

## Typography

The pairing combines the classical cadence of Playfair Display with the clean legibility of Plus Jakarta Sans.

### Typography Rules
- **Editorial Headlines:** Display and Headline roles use Playfair Display in normal to medium weights. Apply italic variations sparingly for poetic focal points (e.g., origin profiles, tasting notes).
- **Body Text:** Uses a lighter weight (300/400) of Plus Jakarta Sans with generous line heights to prevent cognitive fatigue and honor editorial column structures.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy:** Metadata, pricing, notes, and navigation elements utilize all-caps tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.08em` to `0.12em`) at smaller point sizes to balance out the dramatic serif headlines.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts favor an asymmetrical 12-column grid system inspired by printed lookbooks. Content alternates between wide multi-column photographic spans and tightly aligned textual sidebars.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with 64px (`margin`) outer margins and 32px (`gutter`) gutters. Sections make generous use of blank columns (offsetting cards by 1-2 columns) to emulate print layouts.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 32px outer margins, 24px gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 24px (`margin-mobile`) margins, 16px (`gutter-mobile`) gutters. Offsets collapse into stacked linear narratives.

### Spacing Rules
- Vertical whitespace between key editorial sections scales to `space-xl` (or multiples thereof) to isolate stories.
- Elements within a card or collection maintain a tight proportional relationship using `space-xs` and `space-sm`.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids blurred drop shadows and heavy synthetic elevations. Depth is achieved entirely through physical surface layering and precise hairline borders.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Unbleached parchment tone (`#F4EFE7`).
- **Level 1 (Sub-surfaces & Cards):** Pure off-cream tone (`#FAF7F2`) framed with a 1px solid hairline border (`#E5DDD3`).
- **Level 2 (Floating Overlays & Modals):** Translucent base with backdrop filter: `rgba(244, 239, 231, 0.94)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a subtle ambient stroke of `#C8A783` at 40% opacity.
- **Layering Rule:** Use slight overlapping card positions (e.g., negative margins on imagery meeting typography) rather than shadows to express spatial depth.

## Shapes

To mirror architectural stonework, art books, and minimal packaging, the shape language uses crisp 90-degree corners.
